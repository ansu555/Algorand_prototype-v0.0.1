### 3. WaveBreak Token Launchpad

**What it is:** A platform where anyone can create and launch their own cryptocurrency in a fair way.

**Why it matters:** Traditional token launches are often unfair—insiders get cheap prices while regular people pay more. WaveBreak fixes this with a "bonding curve" system that ensures fair pricing for everyone.

**How it works (simple version):**

**Step 1: Create Your Token**
- Choose a name (e.g., "Moon Token")
- Set a symbol (e.g., "MOON")
- Decide how many tokens exist
- Upload a logo and description
- Set your pricing curve

**Step 2: Bonding Curve Phase**

Think of a bonding curve like a vending machine that adjusts its prices based on how much has been sold:
- **Early buyers** pay lower prices (like a launch discount)
- **As more people buy**, prices gradually increase
- **Everyone sees the same price** at any given moment
- **No secret deals** or insider pricing

**Example:**
Imagine launching "Beach Token" with these settings:
- Starting price: $0.01 per token
- Ending price: $0.10 per token
- Goal: Raise 10,000 ALGO

As people buy:
- First 10%: Around $0.02 per token (early bird special!)
- At 50%: Around $0.05 per token
- Final 10%: Approaching $0.10 per token

**Step 3: Anti-Bot Protection**

WaveBreak includes built-in defenses against unfair buying:
- **Cooldown periods:** Must wait ~30 seconds between purchases (bots can't rapid-fire buy)
- **Purchase limits:** Can't buy more than 1% in one transaction
- **Wallet limits:** Each wallet can only own up to 5% of total supply
- **Whale penalties:** Big purchases get reduced rewards

**Step 4: Launch to DEX**

When your funding goal is reached, magic happens automatically:
- 80% of raised money + tokens → Creates a liquidity pool on a DEX
- Pool gets locked for 6 months (anti-rug pull protection)
- Your token becomes tradeable on the open market
- You receive 20% of raised funds as the creator

**Step 5: Early Buyer Rewards**

People who bought during the bonding curve don't receive tokens immediately. Instead:
- They earn "points" that convert to tokens later (1 point = 1 token)
- **Early buyers get bonus points:** 3x multiplier if you buy in first 10%, down to 1x at the end
- **Tokens unlock gradually:** Over 30 days after launch (daily claims)
- This prevents immediate dumping and builds a healthy market

**Real-world comparison:** It's like a Kickstarter campaign for crypto, but with automatic execution and built-in fairness rules.

### 5. Autopilot Trading

**What it is:** Automated trading rules that execute your strategy 24/7 without you needing to watch charts or manually place trades.

**Why it matters:** Nobody can watch the market all day. Autopilot lets you create rules like "buy when price drops 5%" or "rebalance my portfolio weekly," and the system executes them automatically.

**Three Strategy Types:**

#### A) DCA (Dollar-Cost Averaging)

**What it does:** Automatically buys a fixed dollar amount at regular intervals or when conditions are met.

**Example rule:**
"Buy $50 worth of ALGO every time the price drops 5%, but maximum once per day"

**Why use it:** Removes emotion from buying. You get good average prices over time instead of trying to "time the market."

**Real-world comparison:** Like setting up automatic savings from your paycheck, but for crypto.

#### B) Rebalance (Portfolio Balancing)

**What it does:** Keeps your portfolio at target percentages.

**Example rule:**
"Keep my portfolio at 50% ALGO, 30% USDC, 20% USDT. Rebalance when any asset is 5% off target."

**Why use it:** Markets move constantly. One asset might grow to dominate your portfolio, increasing risk. Rebalancing keeps things balanced.

**Real-world comparison:** Like a robotic gardener that keeps all your plants at the right size.

#### C) Rotate (Momentum Trading)

**What it does:** Automatically shifts your holdings into the best-performing assets.

**Example rule:**
"From my list of 5 tokens, always hold the top 3 performers. Check weekly and rotate if rankings change."

**Why use it:** Follows "the trend is your friend" philosophy, automatically riding winners.

**Real-world comparison:** Like automatically moving your money to whichever bank offers the highest interest rate.

**Safety Features:**
- ✅ **Slippage protection:** Won't execute if price moved too much
- ✅ **Cooldown periods:** Prevents over-trading
- ✅ **Max spend limits:** You control how much can be used
- ✅ **Preview mode:** Simulate before going live
- ✅ **Pause anytime:** Full control to stop rules

### Bonding Curves (Fair Token Launches)

**The math (simplified):**

Let's say you're launching a token with:
- Start price: $0.01
- End price: $0.10
- Total supply: 1,000,000 tokens

**Linear curve formula:**
```
Price = $0.01 + ($0.10 - $0.01) × (tokens_sold / total_supply)
```

**Examples:**
- 0% sold: $0.01 + $0.09 × 0 = **$0.01**
- 50% sold: $0.01 + $0.09 × 0.5 = **$0.055**
- 100% sold: $0.01 + $0.09 × 1 = **$0.10**

**Why this is fair:**
- Everyone sees the same price at the same time
- No secret insider prices
- Early supporters are rewarded (lower prices)
- Late buyers can still participate (predictable pricing)

**Anti-bot cooldown:**
- System tracks last purchase block number per wallet
- If (current_block - last_purchase_block) < 10 → Reject
- Bots can't rapid-fire transactions
- Humans unaffected (~30 second wait is reasonable)
